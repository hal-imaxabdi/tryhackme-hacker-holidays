How this went

Between December 1–14, 2025, TryHackMe ran Hacker Holidays — an advent-calendar-style CTF built around a fictional resort, The Byte Lotus. Each day unlocked a new "room": a self-contained challenge spanning OSINT, AI / prompt-injection, cloud misconfiguration, web exploitation, digital forensics, and full boot2root machines.

I worked through 14 rooms across the event, documenting my process end to end — recon, the dead ends, the actual exploitation steps, and the final flag for each one. The write-ups below follow the order I solved them in, from a straightforward OSINT/AI-fingerprint hunt on Day 0 up to a full disk-forensics investigation on Day 14.

Difficulty ramps up as the event goes on: the first few days are Easy recon and web basics, the middle stretch moves into Medium boot2root machines and cloud exploitation, and the last couple of rooms are Hard forensics work reconstructing attacker activity from a compromised device.
